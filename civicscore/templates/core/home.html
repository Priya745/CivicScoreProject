📜 Rules & Guidelines

To maintain fairness, authenticity, and trust in the CivicScore community, please follow these rules carefully.

📍
Geo-Tagged Proof Required

All activity submissions must include photos with location (geo-tag) data enabled. This ensures that your contribution is verified and actually performed at a real location. Add your location in the description section clearly(Important) while submitting an activity.

📷
Relevant & Clear Images Only

Upload only genuine and relevant photos of your activity. Blurry, unrelated, or copied images will be rejected.

🚫
No Fake Submissions

Submitting fake or misleading activities is strictly prohibited. Such actions may lead to deduction of points or account suspension.

⚠️
Account Safety

Repeated violations of rules can result in temporary or permanent blocking of your account to maintain community integrity.

👥
Personal Participation Required

Only upload activities that you have personally performed or participated in. Third-party or internet images are not allowed.

🏆
Fair Reward System

Rewards are granted based on verified activities and accumulated points. Any misuse may result in disqualification from rewards.
